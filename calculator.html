      How working days are counted

      This follows the working day definition in the Agreement for Sale and Purchase of Real Estate (Eleventh Edition 2022, ADLS and REINZ). These days are not working days:

      • Saturdays and Sundays
      • Waitangi Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Anzac Day, King's Birthday, Matariki and Labour Day
      • The Monday after Waitangi Day or Anzac Day when they fall on a weekend
      • 24 December to 5 January inclusive. For a LIM condition, the break runs to 15 January.
      • Nelson Anniversary Day (the Monday nearest 1 February)
      • Any other public holiday the Government declares

      This tool uses the Nelson/Tasman calendar only. Any condition can also be set in calendar days, weeks or as a fixed date. Calendar days and weeks count every day, including weekends and holidays.

      Counting starts the day after the contract date. A working day runs from 9am to 5pm, so a condition is due by 5pm on the date shown.

      Matariki dates are set in law up to 2052. Always check the dates against the signed agreement and the form edition in use.
